Play a motif. Change its code.

This original four-note exercise uses synthesized tones. Listen, tap, or work at your own pace. No Ren recording, voice or lyrics are included. No account or instrument connection is needed.

1. Listen and notice

At 80 BPM, each beat lasts 0.75 seconds. The notes start one beat apart and sound for half a beat. Try changing the tempo: the pitch stays the same while the spacing changes.

Ren’s process-video lessons are a separate, source-verified next step. This exercise makes no claims about his process. “Not stated in this source” remains a valid lesson answer.

2. Edit one thing

Edits stop playback and reset practice. No arbitrary code runs from imported charts.

3. Play or take your time

For timed practice, focus “Tap on a note” and use Space, Enter or touch. For untimed practice, “Next note” demonstrates each note and its action without scoring.
