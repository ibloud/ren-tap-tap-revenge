A fan-made, Tap Tap Revenge-style rhythm game prototype built with vanilla HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript. This game is themed around the music of Ren Gill, featuring mechanics designed to mimic his varied musical styles—from rapid-fire rap verses to emotional, sustained acoustic ballads.

## 🎮 Features

3-Lane Tap Mechanics: Classic rhythm game tapping.
Hold Notes: For sustained acoustic guitar and violin notes. Keep the key held down until the tail ends to score big!
Swipe Notes: Simulating Ren's rapid-fire lyrical flow ("Sick Boi" / "Hi Ren" style). Hit the arrow keys to keep up with the beat!
Dynamic Visuals: A dark, sketch-style aesthetic that reflects the raw emotion of the music.
Combo & Scoring System: Build streaks to multiply your score.

## 🕹️ How to Play

Open index.html in any modern web browser.

Controls:

Tap Notes: A (Left), S (Middle), D (Right)
Hold Notes: Hold A, S, or D until the note trail finishes.
Swipe/Rapid Notes: Left Arrow (Left Lane), Down Arrow (Middle Lane), Right Arrow (Right Lane)
Note: You can also click the lanes with your mouse for Tap notes!

## Optional MIDI control lab

[Connect and learn one pad/button](https://ibloud.github.io/ren-tap-tap-revenge/lesson.html#controller). MIDI input can drive the existing timed tap or untimed Next note in a supporting HTTPS browser after permission. No preset DJ2GO2 numbers are assumed. Held/repeated messages do not retrigger; disconnect/unplug/hidden-page and denied-permission paths preserve touch/keyboard practice. Export/import bounded mappings and review an optional manual test record. No microphone, MIDI output, SysEx, device-ID export, automatic save or upload.

Physical DJ2GO2 testing is open. Tula recording, Shure listening and AirPods Pro 3 accessibility are documented external routes; Creator OS uses local files and reviewed manual notes. See [equipment, accessibility, test instructions and expansion gates](https://github.com/ibloud/tarantula-clone-hero/blob/main/docs/CONTROLLER-LAB.md). Codex assisted this implementation.
