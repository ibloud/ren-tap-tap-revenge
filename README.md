# 🎵 Rhythm & Remedy: The REN Experience

A fan-made, Tap Tap Revenge-style rhythm game prototype built with vanilla HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript. This game is themed around the music of Ren Gill, featuring mechanics designed to mimic his varied musical styles—from rapid-fire rap verses to emotional, sustained acoustic ballads.

## 🎮 Features

3-Lane Tap Mechanics: Classic rhythm game tapping.
Hold Notes: For sustained acoustic guitar and violin notes. Keep the key held down until the tail ends to score big!
Swipe Notes: Simulating Ren's rapid-fire lyrical flow ("Sick Boi" / "Hi Ren" style). Hit the arrow keys to keep up with the beat!
Dynamic Visuals: A dark, sketch-style aesthetic that reflects the raw emotion of the music.
Combo & Scoring System: Build streaks to multiply your score.

## 🕹️ How to Play

Open index.html in any modern web browser.

Controls:

Tap Notes: A (Left), S (Middle), D (Right)
Hold Notes: Hold A, S, or D until the note trail finishes.
Swipe/Rapid Notes: Left Arrow (Left Lane), Down Arrow (Middle Lane), Right Arrow (Right Lane)
Note: You can also click the lanes with your mouse for Tap notes!

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

HTML5 Canvas
Vanilla JavaScript (No dependencies)
